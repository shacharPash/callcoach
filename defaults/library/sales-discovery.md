# Sales discovery

The goal of discovery is to learn whether there is a real, urgent, funded problem you can solve.

- Pain: what breaks today, how often, and what it costs (time, money, risk). Get a number.
- Impact: who feels it and what happens if nothing changes this quarter.
- Current solution: what they use now, what they like about it, why it is not enough.
- Decision: who decides, who signs, who can block, and how they bought the last similar tool.
- Timing and budget: is there a deadline, an audit, a renewal? Is money set aside?
- Next step: agree on a concrete next step with a date before the call ends.

Ask one question at a time, follow up on vague answers ("what do you mean by slow?"), and summarize
what you heard before moving on.
